# 0003: Team-owned camera network

Status: superseded by [0005](0005-cloud-hosted-video-replay-demo.md), 2026-10-03; accepted 2026-10-02.

The first-release camera, the worker machine, and their network belong to the team: a team-bought IP camera and a team-owned worker machine share an isolated network with its own internet uplink (a 4G router), not connected to the university network. This network is the camera network of [0002](0002-live-state-pipeline-and-liff-app.md); the worker only makes outbound HTTPS calls to the API, and frames and footage stay on it. The team holds the camera credentials, and the university permission request covers mounting, power, and the parking-area view, not network or system access. Rejected: an existing university CCTV camera, because the team cannot choose its angle, access to security footage needs a slower approval, and the credentials would not be the team's; a team camera on the university network, because it depends on IT for a port or VLAN and an on-campus machine, the roadmap's main site risk. Accepted cost: the team pays for the hardware and mobile data and is responsible for power, weatherproofing, theft, and remote access, without IT monitoring.
