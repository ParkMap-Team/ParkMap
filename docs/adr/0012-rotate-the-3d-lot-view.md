# Rotate the 3D lot view

Status: accepted, 2026-10-15. Supersedes the "no orbit controls" rule of [0008](0008-build-a-resilient-liff-lot-view.md); the rest of 0008 stands. The 3D view lets people turn the camera around the point it looks at and tilt it between a low oblique view and nearly straight down, as well as pan and zoom. It opens at the 0008 overview, and the reset control returns to it, heading and tilt included. Rotation works by gesture (two fingers on a phone, the right mouse button on a computer), by buttons beside the zoom controls, and by Shift with the left or right arrow key. The 2D plan does not rotate. Turning the camera is a viewpoint change only: it never creates observation evidence, and every status layer stays drawn over buildings, trees, and other static surroundings, so no viewpoint hides or tints a status or turns hidden ground into available space. There is still no entrance-view preset and no north arrow while the calibration is estimated.

The team wants students to see the lot from the side they arrive from, which a fixed angle cannot show, and the static surroundings now give enough cues to stay oriented. The cost is a few more controls and a view people can lose their bearings in, which the reset control answers.
